# Data processing agreement (DPA)

*Last updated: {{updatedAt}}*

This agreement ("**DPA**") is entered into between **qoe.fi** ("**the Processor**") and the creator or media outlet using the Platform ("**the Controller**"), in accordance with **Article 28 GDPR**. It applies automatically to any processing of personal data carried out by qoe.fi on behalf of the Controller.

## 1. Subject and term

The Processor processes personal data to provide the following services: hosting of publications, audience and subscriber management, delivery of newsletters and notifications, reading analytics, subscription and payout management.

The term of processing matches the term of the contractual relationship, plus statutory retention periods.

## 2. Nature, purpose and data categories

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Nature of processing** | hosting, email delivery, statistics, subscription management |
| **Purpose** | provision of the publishing and audience service to the Controller |
| **Categories of data subjects** | readers, subscribers, editorial team members, CRM contacts |
| **Categories of data** | identifiers, contact details (email), preferences, billing data, aggregated reading logs |
| **Special category data** | **none** — the Controller undertakes not to transmit special category data (Art. 9) |

## 3. Processor obligations

The Processor undertakes to:

1. **Process only on documented instructions** from the Controller, including for transfers outside the EU. This agreement and the service configuration constitute that instruction.
2. **Ensure confidentiality**: access limited to authorised personnel bound by an obligation of confidentiality.
3. **Implement the security measures** of Article 32 GDPR (encryption, minimisation, segregation, logging, rate limiting, backups).
4. **Assist the Controller** in responding to data subject rights requests (access, erasure, portability) within **5 working days**.
5. **Notify any data breach** to the Controller **without undue delay** and at the latest **48 hours** after becoming aware of it, with the information needed to notify the supervisory authority.
6. **Assist the Controller** with impact assessments (DPIA) and prior consultations of the authority.
7. **Delete or return** the data at the end of the service, at the Controller's choice, save for mandatory retention obligations (accounting: 10 years; legal evidence: 5 years).
8. **Make available** the information necessary to demonstrate compliance and to allow audits.

## 4. Sub-processors

The Controller authorises the use of the sub-processors listed on the **Sub-processors** page. The Processor:

- informs the Controller of any addition or replacement **at least 30 days** in advance;
- imposes on sub-processors the same obligations as those in this agreement;
- remains liable to the Controller for performance by its sub-processors.

The Controller may object to a change by closing their account, or by requesting a technical alternative where one exists.

## 5. Transfers outside the European Union

Data is hosted in the **European Union**. Any transfer to a third country is governed by an adequacy decision or by the **standard contractual clauses (2021/914)** supplemented with technical measures (encryption, pseudonymisation). The Controller is informed before any new transfer.

## 6. Security and location

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Encryption in transit | TLS 1.2+ |
| Encryption at rest | encrypted disks and backups |
| Segregation | multi-tenant isolation per publication |
| Access | least privilege, audit log, two-factor authentication for the team |
| Backups | encrypted, restore tested |
| Logging | technical logs kept for 12 months |

## 7. Liability

Each party is liable for damage caused by a breach of this agreement, under the conditions of the GDPR (Art. 82) and general law. The Processor is liable for processing carried out on behalf of the Controller; the Controller is liable for the lawfulness of the initial collection and the content of its instructions.

## 8. Fate of the data at the end of the contract

The Controller may **export** all of their audience data (CSV/JSON) and content (Markdown/HTML + files) at any time, including during the notice period. Failing an export, the data is deleted within **90 days** at most after the end of the relationship, backups included.

The Processor may retain data required to comply with a legal obligation (accounting, fraud prevention) for the strictly required period, then delete it.
