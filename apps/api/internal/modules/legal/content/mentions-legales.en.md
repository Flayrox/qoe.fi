## 5. Reporting illegal content

In accordance with European law (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, the "DSA"), any allegedly illegal content can be reported through the **Report** button available on every publication, or by email to [REPORT EMAIL].

A report must state:

1. the identity of the notifier (a natural or legal person);
2. the precise location of the content (URL);
3. the nature of the alleged illegality and the legal grounds;
4. the notifier's good-faith declaration.

An acknowledgement of receipt is sent without delay, and the reasoned decision is communicated as soon as possible. Abusive or manifestly unfounded reports, submitted repeatedly, may lead to the suspension of the reporting feature for the account concerned.
