# Creator agreement

*Last updated: {{updatedAt}}* · *Effective: {{effectiveAt}}*

This agreement supplements the Terms of service as soon as a creator (or a media outlet) **enables monetisation** on qoe.fi. It sets out what you earn, how and when, along with your obligations as an independent publisher.

## 1. Status of the parties

The creator acts as an **independent publisher**. They are not an employee, commercial agent or representative of qoe.fi. They retain full control over their content, their audience and their editorial line.

qoe.fi acts as **technical platform and collection agent**: it collects reader payments on behalf of the creator, allocates them and pays them out as set out below.

| Single-purchase content / one-off support | **[90 %]** | **[10 %]** |

- **Net revenue** means revenue after deducting payment provider fees (Stripe commissions) and VAT collected on the creator's behalf.
- Rates are stated excluding tax. A different rate may be negotiated in a separate contract, in particular with media outlets.
- If payment fees make a transaction unviable (splitting, micro-amounts, exotic currency), qoe.fi may moderate the creator's share of the amount concerned and will inform the creator.

## 3. Payouts and thresholds

- Payouts are made **monthly** after the month's accounts are closed (a safety period to cover refunds, disputes and chargebacks).
- **Payout threshold**: [THRESHOLD — e.g. €50]. Below it, the balance carries over to the next month.

- Payouts are suspended during identity verification (KYC), suspected fraud, an ongoing dispute or non-payment.
- A **detailed monthly statement** is available in the Studio (gross, fees, VAT, net, platform share, creator share).

## 4. Creator tax and compliance

The creator is **solely responsible** for:

- their tax filing and their business status (sole trader, company, association);
- invoicing and the mandatory legal notices provided to readers;
- collecting and remitting **VAT** where applicable (clearly flagged in the Studio);
- their social contributions and any required registration.

qoe.fi may apply withholding required by law (for example the marketplace mechanism for eligible activities) and provides the required information to the authorities. The creator makes available to qoe.fi a collection mandate and a valid tax identifier.

## 5. Editorial obligations

By enabling monetisation, the creator undertakes to:

1. **Describe their offer** faithfully: content included, stated frequency, access conditions.
2. **Actually publish**: deliver value proportionate to the price, promote their publication and moderate their comments section.
3. **Respect their audience**: consent required for all marketing email, a working one-click unsubscribe link in every message, effective removal of unsubscribers.
4. **Comply with the content rules** of the Acceptable use policy.
5. **Respond to readers** within a reasonable time on access or billing problems.

### 5.1. Inactivity

An active paid subscription with **no publication for 90 days** is flagged. After a warning email and a further **30 days** without publication or explanation, qoe.fi may disable new subscriptions and refund subscribers pro rata.

## 6. Audience data

- The creator is the **controller** for their audience data (email addresses, preferences, subscription history), as described in the Data processing agreement.
- qoe.fi hosts that data and makes it available **only** for the purposes of the creator's own publication. Any other use (resale, transfer to another media outlet, cross-enrichment) is prohibited unless the creator has their own legal basis.
- At the end of the relationship, the creator may **export** their audience data. Handling of reader rights requests is coordinated with qoe.fi.

## 7. Brand and communication

- The creator authorises qoe.fi to reference their publication, name, logotype and an excerpt of their content on public discovery pages, in documentation and in non-advertising communications.
- No advertising use of the creator's image is possible without written consent.
- The creator may object to this use by email to [CONTACT EMAIL].

## 8. Suspension, termination and sanctions

- **By the creator**: at any time, effective at the end of the subscribers' current billing period, with subscribers refunded pro rata for their remaining time.
- **By qoe.fi**: in the event of fraud, a clear breach of content rules, failure to meet tax obligations, or repeated damage to reader trust. Except in urgent cases, reasonable notice and an opportunity to respond are provided.
- Amounts due for activity carried out before termination remain payable, subject to disputed amounts and statutory retention obligations.

## 9. Collection mandate

The creator mandates qoe.fi to: issue invoices on the creator's behalf, collect reader payments, refund customers on their behalf, and keep the corresponding records. This mandate ends with this agreement, with payouts remaining due for the prior period.
