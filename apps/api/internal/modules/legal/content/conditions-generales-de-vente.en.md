# Terms of sale — reader subscriptions

*Last updated: {{updatedAt}}* · *Effective: {{effectiveAt}}*

These terms govern the purchase of **paid subscriptions** by a reader ("**the Customer**") from a creator or media outlet publishing on **qoe.fi** ("**the Publisher**").

## 1. Subject and parties

The purchase covers **access to digital content** (articles reserved for paying subscribers, newsletters, archives, audio/video content) published by an independent creator or media outlet on the Platform.

The content supply contract is concluded between **the Customer and the relevant creator**. qoe.fi acts as **technical agent**: hosting, collection through Stripe Payments Europe, access unlocking, subscription lifecycle management and payout of revenue to the creator.

## 2. Price and billing

- Prices are displayed **inclusive of all taxes** for consumers in the European Union, in the currency announced at the time of purchase.
- Applicable VAT is determined automatically from the Customer's country of residence (OSS rules for electronic services).
- The creator may change prices; a change **does not affect** an ongoing subscription before its next renewal, and the Customer is informed by email **at least 30 days** in advance.
- Billing recurs monthly or annually until cancellation.
- If a trial period is offered, it is clearly flagged, with its duration and the price after the trial.

## 3. Payment

- Payment by **bank card** through Stripe Payments Europe. No full card data is stored by qoe.fi: only the card brand, the last four digits and the expiry date are stored, by the provider.
- On a failed payment: automatic retries, email notification, then suspension of access to paid content until the balance is settled. A single failed payment does not cancel the subscription, but a prolonged failure (**30 days**) does.

## 4. Right of withdrawal

### 4.1. Principle

A consumer Customer has **14 days** from subscribing to withdraw, without giving a reason.

### 4.2. Digital content exception

Under Article L. 221-28 of the French Consumer Code, the Customer **loses the right of withdrawal** when requesting immediate access to digital content and that content has been **supplied with their prior express agreement and express waiver** of the right of withdrawal — which the Platform collects before access is opened.

The Customer is therefore invited to check the creator's identity and the description of the offer **before** confirming immediate access.

### 4.3. How to withdraw

For subscriptions whose access has not yet been unlocked, withdrawal is exercised by email to [SUPPORT EMAIL] or from the customer account. Refunds are issued within **14 days** of the request, to the original payment method.

## 5. Term, cancellation, refunds

- **No commitment**: cancellation is available at any time from the customer account. It takes effect at the **end of the already-paid current period**; no further renewal is charged.
- **Content already consumed**: the current period is not refunded pro rata, except for:
  - prolonged technical unavailability attributable to the Platform (more than **7 consecutive days**);
  - billing error or duplicate charge;
  - statutory guarantees (lack of conformity, latent defect).
- **Cancellation by the creator**: if a creator stops publishing paid content, the Customer is informed and refunded pro rata for the remaining period.

## 6. Access to content

- Access is **personal**. Sharing credentials, exhaustively capturing and redistributing paid content is prohibited and may lead to termination without refund.
- Downloading content is only allowed where the creator explicitly offers that feature.
- Content is protected by copyright. Any reproduction, redistribution or resale is prohibited without the creator's authorisation.

- The statutory guarantees of conformity (Art. L. 217-3 et seq. of the French Consumer Code) and for latent defects apply.
- qoe.fi's liability is limited to the technical provision of the service. Editorial responsibility for content lies with the creator.
