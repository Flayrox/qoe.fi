# Acceptable use policy

*Last updated: {{updatedAt}}*

This policy sets out what is **prohibited** on qoe.fi, whether through the interface, the API, newsletters or any other channel of the service. It supplements the Terms of service and applies to every account, including media outlets and third-party integrations.

## 1. Prohibited content

The following are prohibited, in publications as well as in private messages:

1. **Safety of persons**: incitement to violence, credible threats, harassment, doxxing (publishing data that identifies or locates a person), glorification of terrorism or crimes against humanity.
2. **Minors**: any content involving sexual exploitation, including AI-generated depictions. Zero tolerance: immediate report to the authorities and account closure.
3. **Hate and discrimination**: dehumanising speech, promotion of a group's superiority, denial of recognised crimes.
4. **Rights infringement**: counterfeiting, copyright or trademark infringement, invasion of privacy, defamation.
5. **Fraud**: scams, fake reviews, false investment promises, pyramid schemes, identity theft, fake certification badges.
6. **Information manipulation**: content deliberately falsified and circulated as authentic, networks of fake accounts, purchased fake engagement.
7. **Regulated content**: sale of medicines, weapons, drugs, protected species or sexual services, without a legal basis.
8. **Malicious activity**: malware, phishing, chain messages, exploitation of vulnerabilities.

## 2. Prohibited behaviour

- Creating multiple accounts to circumvent a sanction, a quota or a block.
- Impersonating a creator, a media outlet, an institution or a real person.
- Mass data extraction (scraping) or rebuilding a database from the service.
- Disrupting the service: denial of service, automated requests beyond quotas, penetration testing without written authorisation.
- Circumventing billing, rate limits, authentication or access controls.
- Reselling access, or launching a competing service built from qoe.fi data.
- Using the API for spam, cold calling, enriching third-party databases or advertising profiling.

## 3. API and integration rules

- **Personal and media keys**: a key acts only for its publication. Sharing it with an unauthorised third party is prohibited.
- **Scopes**: using a `WRITE` key where a `READ` key would do is bad practice; on compromise, the key must be **revoked immediately**.
- **Rotation**: when rotating, the old key must be removed after the grace period.
- **Public CORS**: the public subscription endpoint (`/v1/publications/{slug}/subscribe`) must be used to collect **voluntary consent**. Injecting purchased lists there is prohibited.
- **Quotas**: respect the documented limits. Repeated overruns may lead to blocking the key, then the account.
- **Webhooks**: always validate the HMAC signature and never reuse a compromised secret.

## 4. Email and audience

- Prior consent is mandatory for all marketing sends (opt-in), and the proof is retained.
- A working **one-click unsubscribe** link in every message (RFC 8058), acted upon immediately.
- Importing addresses that were purchased, rented or collected without a legal basis is prohibited.
- A high complaint rate, trap addresses or excessive bounces lead to sending being suspended.

## 5. Reporting and handling

- Any user can report content (the **Report** button or [REPORT EMAIL]).
- The team acknowledges receipt without delay and applies **graduated measures**: warning, reduced visibility, removal, temporary restriction, suspension.
- Measures are **reasoned** and notified, with an appeal available to [APPEAL EMAIL] (reply within 15 working days).
- Serious cases (child exploitation content, imminent threat) are passed to the competent authorities.

## 6. Sanctions

| Severity | Example | Measure |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Minor | one-off spam, undisclosed commercial mention | warning + removal |
| Moderate | targeted harassment, API quota circumvention | temporary restriction (7 to 30 days) |
| Serious | fraud, repeated rights infringement | account suspension, payouts frozen |
| Critical | child exploitation, terrorism | permanent closure + report to authorities |

Revenue generated by fraudulent activity is not paid out and may be refunded to readers.

## 7. Cooperation with authorities

qoe.fi responds to valid legal requests. Requests concerning data hosted on behalf of a creator are forwarded to that creator, unless legally prohibited.
